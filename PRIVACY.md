# Gallery+ — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 21 September 2026_

> Markdown copy of `store_assets/privacy_policy.html` — keep the two in sync.

This policy explains what data the Gallery+ app ("the app", "we") handles and why. It applies wherever the app is used.

## The short version
- No account, no sign-up.
- No tracking, no analytics, no ads — we don't build a profile of you.
- Your photos stay on your device — except a photo you deliberately choose to edit with AI, which is sent for processing.
- We don't sell or share your data.

## Photos & media
The app asks for permission to access the photos and videos on your device so it can show them inside its gallery, let you open and edit them, and move, copy or delete the ones you select. This access is used only to manage your own media within the app. Photos you don't edit never leave your device.

## AI editing
When you choose an AI feature — Magic Erase, Text to Image, Generative Fill or Upscale — the image you selected (or the part of it being edited) and any text you typed are sent over a secure connection to a third-party AI image-processing service that generates the edited result and returns it to the app.

That image and prompt are used only to produce your result; they are not used to identify you or to train models on your behalf. We do not keep a permanent copy of your photos on our servers — images are processed transiently and results are available only for a short time. Please avoid submitting images that contain sensitive personal information.

## What we don't collect
We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, or advertising identifiers, and we never read your device's live location. The app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs, and we do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with anyone.

## Permissions we use
- **Photos / media** — to display your gallery and let you pick images to view or edit.
- **Media management** (optional, Android 12 and newer) — if you turn it on in Android settings, the app can move and delete photos and videos you select without Android asking for confirmation each time. It gives no access to any other files, and nothing is sent anywhere. You can turn it off at any time; the app then works as before, with Android's confirmation.
- **Motion sensors** — used only on your device for subtle visual motion (parallax) in the interface. This data is never collected or transmitted.
- **Location** — the app never reads where your device is. It reads the GPS coordinates stored inside a photo by the camera that took it, and sends those coordinates to Android's geocoding service to turn them into a place name for the Moments view and the photo details. Android requires the location permission to release a photo's own GPS data to an app. Nothing about your current whereabouts is read, stored or transmitted.

## Children
The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
